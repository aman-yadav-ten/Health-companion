Required Inputs And Clinical Tests

See exactly which profile inputs, clinical values, and medical tests are needed before running each risk assessment.

Shared Profile Fields Age, sex, lifestyle, and medical history

These are entered once in the health profile and reused across all prediction modules.

Home Or Clinic Measurements Height, weight, BMI, and blood pressure

Body measurements can come from a scale, stadiometer, or clinic vitals check.

Lab-Based Clinical Values Glucose, cholesterol, insulin

Cardiovascular Risk

Focuses on blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, BMI, and lifestyle risk factors.

Mandatory App Inputs

Age Gender Height Weight Systolic BP Diastolic BP Cholesterol Glucose Smoking Alcohol Use Physical Activity

Clinical Values Used

  • Blood pressure in mmHg
  • Cholesterol in mg/dL
  • Glucose in mg/dL
  • BMI from height and weight

Exact Test / Source

  • Blood Pressure Measurement using sphygmomanometer or digital BP monitor
  • Lipid Profile / Lipid Panel for cholesterol
  • Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG) or Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)
  • Height and weight measurement during vitals check
Mandatory Tests / Measurements

Exact tests needed: Blood Pressure Measurement, Lipid Profile / Lipid Panel, Fasting Plasma Glucose or Fasting Blood Sugar, and height and weight measurement.

Stroke Risk

Uses glucose, BMI, diagnosis history, and profile-based lifestyle information.

Mandatory App Inputs

Gender Age Hypertension History Heart Disease History Ever Married Work Type Residence Type Average Glucose BMI Smoking Status

Clinical Values Used

  • Glucose in mg/dL
  • BMI from height and weight
  • Confirmed hypertension history
  • Confirmed heart disease history

Exact Test / Source

  • Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG) or Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)
  • Height and weight measurement for BMI
  • Previous hypertension diagnosis, BP record, or doctor note
  • ECG, ECHO, cardiology diagnosis, or prior heart disease record
Mandatory Tests / Measurements

Exact values needed: Fasting Plasma Glucose or Fasting Blood Sugar, height, weight, and confirmed history/records for hypertension and heart disease, plus the non-clinical profile fields.

Diabetes Risk

Uses glucose, blood pressure, BMI, and age first, with optional insulin and skinfold inputs.

Mandatory App Inputs

Pregnancies Glucose Blood Pressure Skin Thickness Insulin BMI Diabetes Pedigree Function Age

Clinical Values Used

  • Glucose in mg/dL
  • Blood pressure in mmHg
  • BMI from height and weight
  • Optional insulin, skin thickness, and family-history score

Exact Test / Source

  • Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG) or Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)
  • Blood Pressure Measurement using sphygmomanometer or digital BP monitor
  • Height and weight measurement for BMI
  • Fasting Serum Insulin, Triceps Skinfold Thickness, and family history intake if available
Mandatory Tests / Measurements

Exact mandatory inputs for the current app: Fasting Plasma Glucose or Fasting Blood Sugar, Blood Pressure Measurement, height, weight, and age. Optional extras: Fasting Serum Insulin, Triceps Skinfold Thickness, pregnancy history, and family history.
